# iShowSpeed reaction clips — Kai Cenat Live (GTA 6, Day 3)

Source: <https://www.youtube.com/live/LHoHInbPkk0> (was live during capture on 2026-11-20).
Captured a 10-minute 720p HLS section, then picked 5 loud/energetic reaction windows
by local audio-spike analysis and rendered them with NeonClipping.

All clips: **1080x1920**, H.264 + AAC, burned-in GTA Neon captions, facecam-heavy
`bottom` layout (large Speed facecam across the lower ~42% of the frame).
Captions were transcribed locally with faster-whisper `small.en` (no data uploaded).
