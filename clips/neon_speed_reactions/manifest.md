## New: Speed + Kai split clip (what was actually asked for)
`06_speed_and_kai_combo.mp4` — **1080x1920, 52 s, H.264 + AAC**.

- **TOP half = iShowSpeed**: shocked "WHAT THE... OH, NO WAY" reaction while a
  car flips in mid-air in GTA 6. Source: `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0jY1GdGP7aw`
  (Top 10 Speed GTA 6 Moments), original Speed stream `qSyo5d-dVzA`.
- **BOTTOM half = Kai Cenat**: amused "oh she's brawling" reaction with gameplay.
  Source: Kai Cenat Live `LHoHInbPkk0` at 08:20–08:46 of the captured section.
- **Playback**: 0–26 s Speed plays while Kai is frozen on his first frame;
  26–52 s Speed freezes on his last frame while Kai plays. Audio follows the
  same order.

Verified programmatically: frozen halves differ by only ~3 MSE units (compression
noise), playing halves differ by ~800–4500 MSE units.

Note: the two reactions are from different GTA 6 scenes (Speed's Day 1 stream,
Kai's Day 3 stream) — the same in-game scene was not available in the captured
material.
