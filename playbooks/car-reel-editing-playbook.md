## VIDEO 1 — Street Drift Montage
**File:** `savefromins.com @street.fighters.crew #turbonight #drift 0 1080P.mp4`
**Technical:** 1916x1078 (LANDSCAPE), 30fps, 17.07s, H.264 6.1 Mbps, AAC stereo 44.1kHz

### Format
- Landscape drift-event montage — multiple cars, one location, golden hour/sunset.
- Raw, energetic "event coverage" feel — polished nahi, street/documentary style.

### Hook (first ~1-2s)
- Direct action: car mid-drift with a HUGE white/blue smoke cloud filling frame, golden sky, "TURBO" event banner in background, a person filming with phone in the smoke.
- Text overlay nahi hook mein — khud action + smoke hook hai.

### Beat structure (hard cuts, ~2-4s each)
1. **~1s** — Silver/white Japanese sedan drifting, massive smoke, "TURBO" banner backdrop, Russian plate, man with phone.
2. **~5s** — Two cars drifting together (silver foreground right + red sedan behind), BIG red bold text **"TOKYO"** across center, vertical banners left, crowd behind barricades, string lights.
3. **~9s** — Red Dodge Challenger drifting, rear 3/4 view, white smoke, big white text **"Ichika"** left-center with slight motion-blur/shadow effect. Background: "DRIFT Night" graffiti on container.
4. **~13s** — Car drifting from behind, big white Cyrillic text **"ИЧИКА"** over rear windshield, car decals **"DRIFT TAXI"** + **"DRIFT EXPO"** on trunk, repeated **"ОНЛИШКОДА"** text on track barriers, marshal on barrels, crowd.
5. **~17s** — Fade to black / end.

### Text overlay technique (SAVE KARNE LAYAK)
- Har beat par EK bada word center ya left-center: pehle red bold ("TOKYO"), phir white with shadow ("Ichika" / "ИЧИКА").
- Font: heavy bold sans-serif, 60-100px equivalent, partially covering the car.
- Effect: halka drop-shadow / motion-blur — text car ke smoke ke saath "melt" hota hai.
- Ye name-tag style hai — driver ya crew ka naam bade aksharon mein.

### Pacing
- Fast: hard cut har ~2-4s. Koi smooth transition nahi, koi cross-dissolve nahi.
- Har cut ek NAYA car / naya angle dikhata hai — repetition nahi.

### Visual layout
- Camera: mostly static/slow-pan, eye-level to low, car ko side mein rakh kar smoke center mein.
- Background layers: crowd + barricades + string lights + banners = depth.
- Color: warm golden sky, dark asphalt, white smoke. Natural grading — heavy color tweak nahi.

### Effort level
- **Low-Medium:** 4-5 clips hard-cut + 3 big text overlays. Koi complex VFX nahi.

### Reuse for user's car videos
- Drift/event footage ho to har clip par ek bada bold word (driver name / car name) lagao.
- Smoke ko hero banao — text smoke ke upar simmer kare.
- 2-3s me cut maro, hard cuts only.
